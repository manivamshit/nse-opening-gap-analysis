Research highlights

  • Nifty 100 stocks opening 1–3% higher had a 50.61% win rate and +0.08% average intraday return.
  • The remaining Nifty 500 stocks in the same gap band had a 47.00% win rate and +0.04% average return.
  • Nifty 100 stocks opening 5–7% higher averaged −0.95% intraday.
  • All 35 observations of gaps ≥10% came from stocks outside the Nifty 100 subset.

Average returns are before costs and are not forecasts. Small sample sizes, especially for large gaps, limit conclusions.

Methodology and limitations

Daily historical NSE equity data, filtered using a current snapshot of Nifty 500 and Nifty 100 constituents. Opening gap = (opening price − previous close) / previous close × 100. Intraday return = (closing price − opening price) / opening price × 100. Each qualifying stock-day is one observation. Assumes purchase at published opening price and sale at published closing price, with no stop-loss.

The analysis excludes brokerage, taxes, slippage, liquidity constraints and transaction costs. Current constituent membership introduces survivorship and look-ahead bias; corporate actions and special sessions have not been independently reconciled. Repeated observations for a stock are not statistically independent. Results are exploratory, not a validated trading strategy. No raw NSE data is distributed with this dashboard.
